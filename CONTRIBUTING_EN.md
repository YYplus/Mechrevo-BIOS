# Contributing

[简体中文](CONTRIBUTING.md) | [English](CONTRIBUTING_EN.md)

Contributions of MECHREVO BIOS / EC firmware and model information are welcome.

For new firmware, provide as much of the following as possible:

- exact model;
- BIOS / EC version;
- original filename;
- official MECHREVO download URL;
- applicable model year or platform;
- known EC pairing information;
- the original official firmware file.

Official firmware binaries may be committed directly under the matching model/platform `firmware/` directory. Preserve the original vendor filename.

Use `unknown` for information that has not been confirmed.
