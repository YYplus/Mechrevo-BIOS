## Known information

- Thanks to @baby170303 for providing model, firmware version and vendor download URL information in [Issue #6](https://github.com/YYplus/Mechrevo-BIOS/issues/6).
- The model year, firmware platform and version are based on the submission. The original N.1.06MRO14 binary has been archived; the two other binaries are not yet archived. Internal metadata and flashing results have not been independently verified.
- Thanks to @baby170303 for contributing `N.2.02MRO23` for the `GMxIR7x` platform in [Issue #11](https://github.com/YYplus/Mechrevo-BIOS/issues/11). The contributor reports requesting `N.1.06MRO14` from customer support on 2026-11-15 and the newer version on 2026-11-26. These are request dates, not BIOS release dates.
- Thanks to @baby170303 for contributing `N.1.04MRO13` for the `GMxxQxC` platform in [Issue #10](https://github.com/YYplus/Mechrevo-BIOS/issues/10). Exact hardware configurations remain unconfirmed; do not cross-flash different platforms based on the marketing model name alone.
